\subsection{The Leading Integrability Defect Heuristic}
Define the leading integrability defect.
Prove it is a lower-bound on the change in dimension to the linearized system.
Thus we establish the category of systems where the heuristic is the valid estimator:
any dynamically feedback linearized system necessarily takes as many steps as the heuristic suggests (possibly more, though).
That is, the heuristic being minimized is a necessary condition for dynamic feedback linearizability.
Point out that the heuristic is (obviously) not the optimal estimator.
Had it been so, a greedy algorithm for dynamic feedback linearization immediately follows.
